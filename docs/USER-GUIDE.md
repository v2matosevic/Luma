# Use Luma

Luma

Open a photo/folder or drop a file. Left/Right and PgUp/PgDn navigate; the mouse wheel zooms at the
pointer, drag pans, F/0 fits and 1 selects actual pixels. R/Shift+R rotate only the view. Space
starts the slideshow and P pauses an animation. I opens Details. Right-click the photo for Copy
picture/file/path, Show in Explorer and Open with. Ctrl+C copies the picture, Ctrl+Shift+C its path,
Ctrl+E reveals it. Formats on this PC distinguishes a registered decoder from a successful sample.


F1 lists shortcuts. Light/dark/follow-Windows themes are in More.
